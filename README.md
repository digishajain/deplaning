# Impact AI Job Board

A dashboard of Senior PM and AI PM roles at AI foundations, tech-for-good,
education-for-all and international development organizations. Each role is
ranked by fit against Digisha's AIPM resume.

- Dashboard: https://claude.ai/artifact/U7LDu6TvAuMDuxQbuz2QAu
- `data/jobs.json`: the roles found so far (seed from 2026-10-26)
- `data/watchlist.json`: organizations to watch for new openings
- `data/actions.json`: certifications and showcase work, linked from each role
- `data/sources.json`: job boards, newsletters and people to follow
- `dashboard/index.html`: the dashboard page
- `config/refresh-routine.md`: matching rules and fit scoring used by the daily refresh
